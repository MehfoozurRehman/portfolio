- File: `buildings.glb` (5.95 MB), 41 roots, **261472 triangles** total (budget 260k tris / 5 MB).
- Scale: adult player 1.65 m. Doors >= 2.05 m, counters 0.95 m, desk 0.75 m, chair seat 0.46 m, railings 1.0 m, single-storey eaves ~2.5-3.0 m.

- `screen` / `label*` quads: material `pal_white` / `pal_cream`, UV (0,0) bottom-left .. (1,1) top-right as seen from the front (standard glTF UVs: use `texture.flipY = false`).
